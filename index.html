Math isn't just numbers — it's the language of patterns, infinity, and the universe. I love fractals (shapes that zoom forever!), the mysteries of outer space, and the fact that some infinities are bigger than others.

Infinity

Saturn's Rings
Saturn's rings are made of billions of ice and rock chunks. They stretch 282,000 km wide but are only ~10 meters thick.
